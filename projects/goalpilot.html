GoalPilot AI

Transform goals into structured roadmaps powered by AI.

GoalPilot AI is an intelligent roadmap generation platform designed to help students and learners plan their journey toward specific career goals. Users simply enter their target role or objective, and the platform generates a structured roadmap powered by AI.

Key Features

  • ✅ AI-generated personalized learning roadmaps
  • ✅ Goal-based career planning
  • ✅ Step-by-step milestones
  • ✅ Curated technology recommendations
  • ✅ Suggested projects and learning resources
  • ✅ Modern responsive dashboard
  • ✅ Clean and intuitive user interface
